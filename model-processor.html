Project Strike Model Processor

Real browser-side validation and conversion for game assets. ZIPs are unpacked locally. GLB/glTF are validated and previewed. FBX is parsed by Three.js and exported as a real binary GLB. Blender files are preserved as source assets and must use the repository's headless Blender conversion workflow.

Choose a model, its texture files, or a ZIP containing the complete asset.

Rules

GLB: preview directly. glTF: keep its BIN/textures beside it or inside the selected ZIP. FBX: parsed and exported through Three.js GLTFExporter. .blend: never fake-converted in the browser; upload it to assets-source/blend/ in the FPS repo and the Blender GitHub Action creates the GLB.

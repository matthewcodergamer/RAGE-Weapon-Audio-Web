Nothing uploads until you press the final button. Mixamo/mocap files are categorized as animations instead of weapon meshes.

FBX: parsed with Three.js, invalid Mixamo morph metadata is repaired, then exported as binary GLB. GLB: validated unchanged. glTF: BIN/textures are resolved then exported to GLB. BLEND: goes through the FPS repo headless Blender workflow. Failed conversion is never renamed into a fake GLB.
